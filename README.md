# Bubble Tsek website

Bubble sheet checker for teachers. Open it in any phone or computer browser, or add it to your home screen.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages (free)

1. Sign in at https://github.com. Click **+ → New repository**.
2. Name it `bubble-tsek-web`, choose **Public** (Pages is free for public repositories), and click **Create repository**.
3. Click **uploading an existing file**. Drag in **everything inside** this folder, then click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under **Branch**, choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then click **Save**.
5. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh. Your link appears at the top, like
   `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/bubble-tsek-web/`

## On your phone
- Open the link in **Chrome** (Android) or **Safari** (iPhone).
- Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (Safari: **Share → Add to Home Screen**). It opens like an app and works offline.
- Tap **Allow** when it asks for the camera.

## Updating
Upload the changed files to the same repository. In `sw.js`, change `bubbletsek-v1` to `bubbletsek-v2` so phones get the new version.

Your saved scores stay on each phone/browser where you use the site. Nothing is sent to any server.
