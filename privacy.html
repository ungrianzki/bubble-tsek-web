Bubble Tsek – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Bubble Tsek is a bubble sheet checker used by a teacher to score quizzes and exams.

What data the app uses

Class lists (student names and roll numbers), exam answers, scores, and pictures of scanned bubble sheets. This data is stored on the teacher's own device.

Google Drive

If the teacher turns on Google Drive sync, the app uses the drive.file permission to save its own files (class data, scores and sheet pictures) into the teacher's Google Drive. It can only see files that Bubble Tsek created. It cannot read any other files in the Drive. The teacher's email address is used only to show which account is signed in.

Sharing

Bubble Tsek has no server of its own. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It goes only to the teacher's device and the teacher's own Google Drive.

Removing data

Delete data in the app (My classes), or delete the "Bubble Tsek" folder in Google Drive. Access can be removed anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
